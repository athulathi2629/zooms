# Zoom Cards – Palakkad | Card Printing Press & Custom Designing

An elegant, mobile-responsive showcase website and interactive cost estimator created for **Zoom Cards – Palakkad**, a premier **card printing press with in-house custom designing**, styled with a signature **Red & White** visual theme with subtle warm gold accents.

---

## 🏛️ Business Overview Grounded in Palakkad

- **Business Name**: Zoom Cards
- **Nature of Business**: **Card Printing Press with In-House Custom Designing** (Wedding cards, greeting cards, offset packaging, ID cards, certificates, and commercial prints).
- **Established**: 2012 (14+ Years of Trust & Craftsmanship)
- **Directory Ratings**: 4.5 – 4.9 on Justdial, WeddingBazaar, and Google Reviews
- **Printing Press Location**:
  - **Main Printing Press & Design Facility**: Near Ninan Complex, Mettupalayam Street, Tharekkad, Palakkad – 678001, Kerala
  - *Working Hours*: Opens 09:30 AM onwards (Mon – Sat)
- **Core Offerings**:
  - **Custom Card Designing**: In-house creative DTP design in Malayalam, Tamil, and English with personalized monograms, traditional Kerala motifs, and proofing.
  - **Card Printing Press**: Factory-direct offset printing, foil stamping, laser die-cutting, hardbound boxes, acrylic cards, and envelopes.
  - **Allied Printing**: PVC ID cards, parchment certificates, flex banners, diaries, packaging cartons, and printing inks.
- **Payment Terms**: 60% upon booking/proof approval & 40% on event date/final handover.
- **Accepted Payment Modes**: **UPI & Cash only** (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI, instant QR scan, and Cash at Press). *Cheques and demand drafts are not accepted.*

## 🎨 Design System & Features

- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: Crimson & Ruby Red (`#B71C1C`, `#C62828`, `#7F0000`)
  - Accent: Auspicious Warm Gold (`#C59B27`, `#FFE082`)
  - Clean Contrast: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Soft Pearl (`#FAFAFC`, `#FFF0F2`)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: *Cinzel* (Google Fonts) – Luxury serif ideal for card press craftsmanship.
  - Body: *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Google Fonts) – Highly legible modern UI typography.
- **Interactive Features**:
  - **Live Wedding Card Cost Calculator**: Real-time pricing calculation based on card style, quantity slider (100–1,500 cards), and luxury finishes (gold foil, monogram envelopes, bilingual Malayalam/English typesetting & designing, wax seals).
  - **Automatic Payment Schedule Split**: Dynamically calculates the exact 60% booking advance and 40% final delivery payment (via UPI / Cash).
  - **One-Click WhatsApp Quote Generator**: Formats and pre-fills an itemized enquiry message with the customer's selected card style, quantity, add-ons, and Tharekkad printing press reference.
  - **Filterable Solutions Catalog**: Switch seamlessly between Wedding Cards, ID Cards, Certificates, Offset Packaging, and Banners.
  - **Tharekkad Printing Press Showcase**: Direct WhatsApp links, hours, complete address, landmark, and Google Maps direction links.
  - **Online Enquiry Modal**: Accessible `<dialog>` modal with validation and instant WhatsApp handover for card design & print requests.
  - **Mobile Sticky Action Bar**: Instant access to WhatsApp quotes, calculator, and press location on mobile screens.
  - **Print Stylesheet**: Clean printable estimate output (`Ctrl + P` or "Print Estimate" button).

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can preview the website immediately using any standard web browser or by serving it through Python:

### Option 1: Direct File Preview
Simply double-click or open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox:
```powershell
Start-Process "C:\Users\shaju\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\zoom-cards-palakkad\index.html"
```

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Python)
Run the following in PowerShell:
```powershell
cd C:\Users\shaju\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\zoom-cards-palakkad
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then open [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) in your browser.
